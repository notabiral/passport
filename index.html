For travellers on weaker passports, the question is never just where to go, it is which borders will let you in. Switch passports and the whole ledger switches with you: where you can go, where another visa in your passport opens a door, and where you need a visa even to change planes. Every entry links to its source and shows the date it was last checked. If an entry could not be verified, it is left out rather than guessed.

Travelling on

Rules change. Always re-check the source linked on each entry, and confirm with your airline, before you book. This site is informational only.

How this works. Tourism and short visits only, ordinary passports only. Henley Passport Index scores shown under each passport are display context only, July 2026. Entries come from official government sources wherever they could be read; a small number cite Wikipedia and say so on the entry. Transit entries are official sources only. See DATA_POLICY.md and CONTRIBUTING.md.

Inspired by The Nepali Passport Diaries by Rashika Karki, generalised here to multiple passports. No data was copied from that site; every entry here was researched fresh for its own passport.
