How this works. Tourism and short visits only, ordinary passports only. Henley Passport Index scores are display context only, July 2026. Entries come from official government sources wherever they could be read; a small number cite Wikipedia and say so on the entry. Transit entries are official sources only. See DATA_POLICY.md and CONTRIBUTING.md. Informational only.

Inspired by The Nepali Passport Diaries by Rashika Karki, generalised here to multiple passports. No data was copied from that site; every entry here was researched fresh for its own passport.
